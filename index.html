Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Hype Arşivi Uploader (“the App”) is a personal desktop tool operated by the owner of the “Hype Arşivi” channel. It creates short vertical gaming highlight videos from the operator's own or permitted livestreams and uploads them to the operator's own TikTok account. The App is not offered to the public.

Terms of Service

1. Who may use the App

The App is used only by its operator to post to the operator's own TikTok account. No other users can sign up for or log in to the App.

2. Content

  • Only videos from the operator's own streams, or streams the operator has permission to use, are posted.
  • All posted content must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • The App does not add watermarks, promotional overlays or third-party branding to videos.

3. Use of TikTok

The App uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. It does not automate the TikTok website or app, and it does not bypass any TikTok limits.

4. No warranty

The App is provided “as is” for personal use, without warranties of any kind.

5. Changes

These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

1. Data the App accesses

  • The operator's own TikTok account access token and open ID, obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  • Basic profile information returned by TikTok (display name), used only to show which account is connected.
  • The video files and captions the operator chooses to post.

2. How data is used

Data is used only to upload and publish the operator's videos to the operator's own TikTok account and to show the result of each upload in the App.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer, in a protected folder, and are never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. The App has no backend server and no database outside that computer.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

5. Third-party data

The App does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.

6. Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Manage app permissions). Disconnecting the account in the App deletes the locally stored token.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the operator through the “Hype Arşivi” channel.
